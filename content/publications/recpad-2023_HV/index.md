abstract: |
  Electric grid asset inspections are critical to keep modern society dependent on a stable, uninterrupted electricity supply, but growing image volumes bottleneck on human interpretation. This initial investigation tests a deep learning model for identifying defects in visible-light images of power line insulators, using a public dataset of 1,688 images with over 6,000 shell insulators and a Faster R-CNN on Detectron2. It achieves a mean average recall of 82.6% and mean average precision of 79.9% (mAR/mAP@50:95) across the three classes.

## Overview

Initial investigation into Faster R-CNN (Detectron2) defect detection on visible-light power line insulator images, covering good, broken, and flashover shell classes with test-time augmentation.
