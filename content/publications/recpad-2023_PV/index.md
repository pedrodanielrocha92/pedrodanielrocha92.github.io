abstract: |
  Periodic inspection of electrical grid assets is fundamental to manage maintenance and guarantee safe, reliable grid operation, but the huge amounts of collected image data make automated inspection mandatory. This paper investigates a deep learning approach to automated classification of defects in operating photovoltaic modules from outdoor thermography images, testing several variants of a simple architecture on a public infrared dataset with 12 classes. Classification reaches Precision, Recall, and F1-score of 0.87, 0.86, and 0.86 respectively, demonstrating the practical value of the automated approach.

## Overview

Simple deep learning architectures for 12-class defect classification of operating photovoltaic modules from UAV-collected outdoor thermographic images, with rotation/mirror augmentation balancing 150 images per class.
