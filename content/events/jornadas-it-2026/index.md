I had the opportunity to attend Jornadas IT 2026, focused on Space, Defense, and Emerging Technologies.

### Research and Emerging Technologies

The event highlights ongoing research and development in resilient communications, sensing, surveillance, monitoring, and artificial intelligence. It reinforces the importance of interdisciplinary approaches to addressing challenges in security and critical infrastructure.

I also gained a deeper appreciation for the role of Instituto de Telecomunicações as a leading research institute, and I am grateful for the opportunity to contribute within such a prestigious and impactful environment.

### Poster Session

During the poster sessions, I connected with researchers and exchanged ideas around my work on “Strengthening Power Infrastructure Resilience through Autonomous Inspection Systems.”

As infrastructure becomes increasingly complex and critical, autonomous systems and AI will play an important role in improving resilience and reliability.

### Reflection

It is always valuable to step outside day-to-day work and engage with different perspectives.
