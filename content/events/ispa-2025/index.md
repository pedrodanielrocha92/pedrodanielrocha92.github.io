I’m pleased to share my contribution to the 14th International Symposium on Image and Signal Processing and Analysis (ISPA 2025), held in Coimbra.

### Conference Contribution

I contributed to the organization of the symposium, gaining a different perspective on the planning and coordination behind a scientific conference. Alongside this role, I presented our paper, “Automating Electrical Grid Asset Inspections Using Deep Vision: Evaluating the Impact of Annotation Detail Level,” which was selected as a runner-up for the Best Student Paper Award.

### Study Summary

The study follows a data-centric approach to investigate how annotation consistency, spatial granularity, and categorical definition affect deep learning models for visual inspection. The results show that missing annotations penalize recall, while tighter spatial annotations favour precision.
