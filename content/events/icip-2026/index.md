### Study Summary

Annotating large-scale datasets for automated electrical grid inspection can be labor-intensive. Our work exploits the different impacts of annotation errors on defective and non-defective classes to reduce manual annotation effort while preserving accurate defect supervision. We propose a semi-supervised pipeline that combines partial human annotations, model-assisted labeling, supervision-aware label merging, instance-based image cropping, and prompt-based segmentation.
